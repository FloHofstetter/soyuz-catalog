## Context

What is the situation that calls for a decision? Include the constraints,
the requirements, and the relevant prior art. Keep it short — one or two
paragraphs. If the context is large, link to a design doc instead of pasting
it here.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences. Be unambiguous: a future reader
should be able to act on this without rereading the rest of the ADR.

## Consequences

What changes as a result of this decision? Include both the upside and the
cost. Specifically:

- **Positive:** what becomes easier or possible.
- **Negative:** what becomes harder, more expensive, or constrained.
- **Neutral:** notable side effects that are neither pro nor con.

## Alternatives considered

Brief — one paragraph per alternative — and why it was rejected. The point
is to prove the decision was made deliberately, not to write an essay on
each option.
